Privacy Policy

RawTalent HeartBeat — Last updated 3 September 2026

1. Who we are

RawTalent HeartBeat ("HeartBeat") is an internal tool built and operated by RawTalent Recruitment ("RawTalent", "we", "us") for use by our own staff and workforce partners. It is not a public product or service, and is not available for signup by the general public.

2. What this page covers

This policy explains how HeartBeat handles personal data, including data accessed through optional Google integrations (such as "Sign in with Google" and Calendar Sync). It applies only to HeartBeat itself, not to RawTalent's recruitment services generally.

3. What data we collect and why

Depending on your role, HeartBeat may hold:

  • Staff account details (name, work email, role) — to operate logins and permissions.
  • Work records relevant to RawTalent's recruitment and childcare-staffing operations (e.g. centre, booking, and candidate information; call notes; tasks) — to run day-to-day operations.
  • If you choose to use "Sign in with Google": your name, email address, and profile photo, used only to identify your account.
  • If you choose to connect Calendar Sync: read/write access to the Google Calendar of the account you connect, used only to keep RawTalent booking events in sync with that calendar. We do not access any other Google service, and do not read or store the content of unrelated calendar events beyond what is needed to perform the sync.

4. How we use Google user data

Any data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the specific HeartBeat feature you enabled it for (identifying your login, or syncing booking events to your calendar). We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with any third party except the service providers below who help us run HeartBeat.

5. Where data is stored

HeartBeat's data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) and processed on Railway (application hosting). Some features use Anthropic's Claude AI to generate summaries or answer questions from RawTalent's own internal content — these requests are not used to train Anthropic's models.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain data for as long as your account is active and as needed for our business records. You can disconnect Calendar Sync or revoke "Sign in with Google" access at any time (from HeartBeat's Settings, or from myaccount.google.com/permissions) — this immediately stops any further access to your Google account. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Access to HeartBeat requires an individual login, and permissions are scoped by role. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). We restrict who can access production data to authorised RawTalent staff.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to contact@rawtalent.com.au.

This policy is written in plain language for an internal tool with a small, known user base, and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Treat it as a starting draft rather than a legal document until it's had that review.
